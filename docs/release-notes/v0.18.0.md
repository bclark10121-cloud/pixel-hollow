# Pixel Hollow v0.18.0

Release 7: the standards review pieces every game now shares. Records with your 18 stamps and your stats, keys and buttons you can change, Run with Hold or Toggle, and sound through the shared mixer.

## Five minute test

1. Install the APK over v0.17.0, or open the game page and press Play in your browser. Your saves stay. The start screen shows Version 0.18.0.
2. Press Continue and walk around for a minute.
3. Open the Menu. Records sits before Title. Press it. You see the 18 stamps, then your stats: play time, treasures, sets, stamps, hearts, days and photos. Stamps you already had show as Earned.
4. Earn a stamp, for example find something after dark. The game says "New stamp" as before, and Records shows it with today's date.
5. Open Settings, then Change controls. Pick Journal and press another key. Play and press the new key: the journal opens. Pick Map and press Y on a controller: Y opens the map now.
6. On the same page, set Run style to Toggle. Press Shift or RB once to run and again to stop. Then press Reset controls twice.
7. Settings, Backup and restore: copy the code, then restore it. Your treasures stay.

## New in this version

- Records in the Menu: your 18 stamps as achievements and 7 stats on one page (G4.10, G4.11). A stamp and its achievement are earned at the same moment.
- Change controls in Settings: every play key and the Act, Journal, Map and Run buttons (G5.22). Arrows, Enter, Esc, B, Menu and View always work in menus. Your old button choices carry over.
- Run style: Hold or Toggle, for the keyboard and the controller (G5.23). Run now has a key too: Shift.
- How to play names the keys you picked.
- Old saves are now tested every release (G5.24).
- Copy bug report in Tester tools adds the last ten errors from this device (G5.27).
- Sound goes through the shared mixer (G5.28). The Music and Sound sliders work as before.

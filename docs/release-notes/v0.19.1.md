# Pixel Hollow v0.19.1

Pixel Hollow's own gameplay tune. It used to share its in-game tune with By Lantern's Glow. Now it has a new one in a different key, at a quicker pace, with a brighter lead.

## Five minute test

1. Install the APK over v0.19.0. Your saves stay.
2. Open the game on Android. The title track "Airship Serenity" starts with no tap.
3. Tap Play and your slot. The title track fades and the new Pixel Hollow tune starts.
4. Open By Lantern's Glow and play. Its tune is different from Pixel Hollow's.
5. Turn Music off in Settings. Both tunes stay silent.

## New in this version

- A new gameplay tune for Pixel Hollow in F major (G5.21, asked for by BC: different music for each game's start screen and gameplay).
- The start screen still plays "Airship Serenity". Nothing else changed.

## Shared kit

Kit 1.14.0, unchanged.

Version 0.19.1 installs over 0.19.0 and keeps your saves.

## Tested and not tested

Tested on the cloud test machine: a browser check that the title track plays on the start screen, stops on Play and the new tune runs after it, the split check and the standards suite. The signed APK was checked for its signature, the tester gate and no internet permission.

Not tested: a real phone or the Retroid. Listen once to confirm you like the new tune.

## Caveat

The tune is made in code, so it sounds like a simple chiptune. If you want a Patch.gg track for gameplay instead, say so and I will pull three options.

# Pixel Hollow v0.19.0

Release 8: Back at the top left of every menu panel, a check that every screen fits upright and sideways, and the start screen's own title track, "Airship Serenity".

## Five minute test

1. Install the APK over v0.18.0. Your saves stay.
2. Open the game on Android. The title track "Airship Serenity" starts with the start screen, no tap needed. In a browser it starts on the first tap.
3. Tap Settings. Back sits at the top left. Scroll the list: Back stays put. Tap Back.
4. Tap Play and your slot. The title track fades and the game's own tune comes back.
5. Open the Menu, then the Journal, the Map and Records. Each has Back at the top left.
6. Hold the phone upright, then sideways. Every panel fits both ways.

## New in this version

- Back at the top left of Settings, the slot list, How to play, Records, Change controls, the Journal, the Map, the museum, the picker, backup and restore and the photo album. Your character keeps Done, now at the top left. Back stays put while a long list scrolls.
- The Journal's page buttons read Prev and Next, so they are not mistaken for Back.
- Start screen music (G5.21): "Airship Serenity" by Kevin MacLeod, picked by BC on 2026-11-17. No two games share a title track.
- On Android the music starts with the start screen. In a browser it starts on the first press.

## Shared kit

Kit 1.14.0: the web header with Back at the top (`BC.header`) and the title track player (`BC.titleTrack`).

## Install

Version 0.19.0 installs over 0.18.0 and keeps your saves.

## Test checklist

- [ ] Back at the top left of every panel, never under Android's bottom bar.
- [ ] A controller's first press in a panel lands on the first row. Up from there reaches Back.
- [ ] Android: the title track plays with no tap. Music Off keeps it silent.
- [ ] Upright and sideways on a phone and the Retroid: every panel fits.

## Tested and not tested

Tested on the cloud test machine: the fit check at 12 screen sizes upright and sideways (0 problems), the controller-only run, the standards suite (title card, slots, Settings, Menu, pause, Tester tools, test hooks), the kit checks and the title track in Chrome (plays on the start screen, stops on Play). The signed APK was checked for its signature, the tester gate and no internet permission.

Not tested: a real phone or the Retroid. A browser that cannot play Ogg Vorbis (older iPhones) keeps the game's own tune on the start screen.

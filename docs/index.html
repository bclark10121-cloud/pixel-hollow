What kind of game is this?

No combat. No enemies, no health bars, no boss fights.
No limits. Unlimited bag. No energy bar. No timers.
No way to lose. A miss never costs you anything.
Finding is the reward. Treasures, fish, bugs, crops, recipes and friends.

Things to do

  • Play in any of five save slots. The game saves by itself.
  • Explore six regions and fill your journal: meadows, autumn woods, a cave, a Deep Cave with an underground lake, snowy peaks and a desert canyon.
  • Fish, catch bugs and grow a garden.
  • Donate sets to the museum, then walk its hall and see every case.
  • Take photos with filters in photo mode.
  • Play fetch with your companion and see what it brings back.
  • Pin a missing treasure and follow the hint.
  • Decorate your cabin, cook on your stove and share dishes with neighbors.
  • Join a festival every season and visit the traveling merchant.
  • Befriend the hen, the duck and the goat.

Controls

Keyboard
WASD or arrows walk. E, Space or Enter acts. J opens the journal and M the map. Esc or P opens the Menu. F switches full screen. Settings and How to play list every key.
Xbox controller
Left stick or D-pad walks. A acts. X opens the map and Y the journal. RB runs. View or Menu opens the Menu. In menus A chooses and B goes back. Settings can change the buttons.
Touch
Drag anywhere to walk, or pick Tap to walk in Settings. Tap A to act. The Menu button at the top right opens the journal, the map, Settings and the rest.

Play with an Xbox controller

Any Xbox Wireless Controller with Bluetooth works. That is every Series X|S controller and the Xbox One controllers made since 2016. If the plastic around the Xbox button matches the face of the controller, it has Bluetooth. Other controllers that your phone or browser sees as a standard gamepad use the same buttons.

On Android
  1. Hold the Xbox button to turn the controller on.
  2. Hold the small Pair button on top for 3 seconds, until the Xbox button flashes fast.
  3. On your phone, open Settings, then Connected devices, then Pair new device.
  4. Tap Xbox Wireless Controller. The Xbox button stays lit when it is connected.
  5. Open Pixel Hollow. The touch buttons hide as soon as you use the controller.
In your browser
  1. Connect the controller to your computer with a USB cable or Bluetooth.
  2. Open Play in your browser. Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari all work.
  3. Press any button once after the game loads, so the browser sees the controller.

Install on Android

  1. Tap Download for Android.
  2. Open the downloaded file.
  3. If Android asks, allow installs from this source.
  4. Tap Install, then open Pixel Hollow. Version 0.16.0 installs over 0.15.0 and keeps your save. It installs next to the old Helena Hollow app and does not replace it.

Android may show a Play Protect notice for apps from outside the Play Store. Want your Helena Hollow finds? Copy a backup code in the old app and import it in Settings here.
